# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Tindah community welcoming and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability,
appearance, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with useful context.
- Respect privacy, consent, boundaries, and different levels of experience.
- Help de-escalate conflict and make space for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention, including unwanted advances.
- Trolling, insults, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

These expectations apply in repository activity, project communication, events,
and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to project maintainers, with links, dates, screenshots,
and context when safe. Do not expose reporters or sensitive details publicly.
Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, impose temporary restrictions, or
permanently exclude participants. They will avoid conflicts of interest, protect
confidentiality as far as practical, and prohibit retaliation.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and applies to contributors,
maintainers, users, and guests.
